This website is a front-end demonstration. Submitting an order records it in local browser storage and does not notify a restaurant or guarantee that food will be prepared.

Menu & prices

Menu items, descriptions and prices are examples in Ghana cedis. Availability, ingredients and prices must be confirmed by the restaurant before a real purchase.

Allergens & dietary needs

Guests should tell restaurant staff about allergies or dietary requirements. This demo cannot verify ingredients or prevent cross-contact.

Privacy

Order details are stored in local storage on the browser used. They are not sent to a server by this demo. Avoid entering sensitive personal information.
